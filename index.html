My Story

My name is Abdi Dadu Woya. I was born in 1999 in Batu City to my mother, Amane Mieso, and my father, Dadu Woya.

I began my kindergarten education at the age of two and continued my elementary education at Aleelu Primary School. After successfully passing the Grade Eight Certificate Examination with good results, I continued my secondary and preparatory education at Meda Hayota School.

After completing my preparatory education, I took the university entrance examination and achieved good results. As a result, I joined Jimma University in 2018 E.C. (Ethiopian Calendar).

Currently, thanks to Almighty Allah, I am a Computer Science student at Jimma University's Agaro Campus. I am working hard to improve my knowledge, develop my skills, and prepare myself for a successful future in the field of technology.

I believe that education, hard work, patience, and determination are the keys to success. My goal is to complete my university education successfully, become a competent computer science professional, and use my knowledge and skills to contribute positively to the development of my community and my country.

Finally, I would like to thank Almighty Allah, my parents, my teachers, and everyone who has supported me throughout my educational journey. Their encouragement and support have played an important role in helping me reach this stage of my life.

My motto:

Education, hard work, patience, and determination are the keys to success.
